# Arabic content writing

Write in natural, clear Arabic suited to the requested audience and channel.
Use Modern Standard Arabic by default; match a requested dialect when
appropriate.

- Identify the intended reader, purpose, and desired action.
- Put the useful information first and prefer concrete wording over slogans.
- Keep terminology, tone, and formatting consistent.
- Preserve factual details supplied by the user; do not invent credentials,
  promises, statistics, or quotations.
- Adapt length and structure to the medium.

Ask for essential missing context only when a reasonable draft cannot be made
without it.
