# Summarization

Summarize only the material provided or verified. Preserve names, numbers,
dates, decisions, dependencies, and caveats that affect the meaning.

- Lead with the main conclusion or purpose.
- Group related details and remove repetition.
- Keep evidence distinct from interpretation.
- Do not add facts, recommendations, or implications absent from the source.
- Match the requested length and audience.

If the source is ambiguous or incomplete, retain that uncertainty rather than
silently resolving it.
