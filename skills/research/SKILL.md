# Research and evaluation

Answer research questions with a clear trail from evidence to conclusions.

1. Define the question and what evidence would answer it.
2. Prefer primary, current, and directly relevant sources when browsing is
   available.
3. Compare claims across sources and note publication dates where they matter.
4. Separate verified facts, reasoned conclusions, and uncertainty.
5. Link or name sources for material claims; never fabricate citations.
6. Summarize limitations and what additional evidence could change the result.

If live research tools are unavailable, say so and provide a clearly labeled
knowledge-based answer instead of implying a search was performed.
