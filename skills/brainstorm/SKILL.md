# Brainstorming

Explore useful options for the user's goal without prematurely converging on a
single idea.

- Generate distinct directions rather than rephrasing one concept.
- Tie each idea to the audience, constraints, and desired outcome.
- Include at least one low-risk practical option and, when appropriate, one
  more ambitious option.
- Note trade-offs, assumptions, and the smallest experiment that can validate
  a promising idea.
- Recommend a shortlist only after comparing the options.

Avoid generic filler, unsupported claims, and ideas that ignore stated
constraints.
